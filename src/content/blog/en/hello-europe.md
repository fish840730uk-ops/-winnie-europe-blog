Hi, welcome to my blog.

This is where I'll be writing about everyday life and work in Europe — office culture differences, the ups and downs of finding an apartment, and the odd city scene I happen to snap on weekends. Hopefully these words and photos let you experience a bit of this journey with me.

## Why this blog

It started as a way to keep memories, but I realized organizing these experiences could also help anyone thinking about living or working in Europe — so I decided to share it publicly.

More cities, stories, and photos are coming soon.
