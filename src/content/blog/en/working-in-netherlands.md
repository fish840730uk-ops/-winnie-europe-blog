During my time working from Amsterdam, a few things stood out compared to the office culture back in Taiwan.

## Direct communication

Dutch colleagues will say "I disagree" straight to your face in a meeting. It caught me off guard at first, but it's just how they communicate — direct, not personal.

## Leaving on time is the norm

By 5pm the office is nearly empty. People draw a clear line between work and personal time.
